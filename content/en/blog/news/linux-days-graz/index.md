On April 10 and 11, COHIRADIA participated in the [Graz Linux Days](https://www.linuxtage.at/de/) with an [information booth](https://www.linuxtage.at/de/infostaende-2026/#cohiradia). The Graz Linux Days are Austria’s largest Linux conference, attracting several hundred participants. Visitors to the booth had the opportunity to experience the entire COHIRADIA system
in action. 

<img src="/../../../de/blog/2026/02/18/cohiradia-nimmt-an-den-linux-tagen-graz-teil/SEL_20260411_104722_cut.png" style="max-width: 80%; height: auto;"/>

<br><br/>
**The highlight:** Visitors could admire the platform in all currently supported configurations:

<div style="clear: both;"></div>

* Classic setup with PC and COHIWizard
* Miniature version with the Raspberry Pi and COHI-Player mini by CP. Gallenmiller. 

Both systems were connected, on the one hand, to a classic tube receiver from the 1950s via cable
and, on the other hand, to two transistor portable radios from the 1960s via a  small low power transmitter with loop antenna .
The signal converters used were the Red Pitaya STEMLAB and the OSMO-fl2k USB/VGA converter. This allowed the entire range of COHIRADIA systems implemented to date to be viewed at once.
<br><br/>

Interest was relatively high; many visitors, among them many young people, were interested in the system. The audience was very diverse; of course, there were also amateur radio operators in attendance, and all participants were united by their shared interest in Linux applications.
