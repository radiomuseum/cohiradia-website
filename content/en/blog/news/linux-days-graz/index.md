On April 10 and 11, COHIRADIA participates in the [Graz LINUX Days](https://www.linuxtage.at/de/). 
So if you have time and interest, you will have the opportunity to experience the entire COHIRADIA system 
in operation at this [info booth](https://www.linuxtage.at/de/infostaende-2026/#cohiradia). The special feature: You can admire the platform in all variants supported so far:

<div style="clear: both;"></div>

* Classic setup with PC and COHIWizard
* Miniature version with Raspberry Pi and COHI-Player mini by C.P. Gallenmiller.
Both systems will be connected there, on the one hand with a classic tube receiver from the 50s via cable
and on the other hand with a transistor portable radio from the 1960s via a small loop antenna.
As playback units, both the Red Pitaya STEMLAB and the OSMO-fl2k USB/VGA converter will be used.
This means the entire range of COHIRADIA systems implemented so far will be visible at once.

If you happen to be nearby and want to get first-hand information, this is a good opportunity.
Amateur radio groups will also be present at the same event.

Small special: Right at the neighboring booth, you can admire a fully functional low-field MRI device built at TU Graz,
and possibly even have a scan of your own brain made there.
What this device has in common with COHIRADIA is the RedPitaya STEMLAB, which is used as the central signal converter in both projects.
