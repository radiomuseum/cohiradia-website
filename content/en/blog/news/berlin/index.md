On 30.08.2025, the ["Long Night of Museums in Berlin"](https://langenachtdermuseen.berlin/event/radio-senden-radio-empfangen-4f074f88/?utm_source=nl&utm_medium=om&utm_campaign=lndm25&utm_content=nl5) took place at the German Museum of Technology in Berlin. There, among other things, COHIRADIA could be admired in real operation. Claus-Peter Gallenmiller presented not only the well-known PC version but also the newest variant "COHI-Player mini" developed by him, which is optimized for exhibitions and museums based on the Raspberry Pi. The 'Mini' works without manual operation and generates a complex MW spectrum immediately after switching on. Thanks to Claus-Peter Gallenmiller's initiative, this player will now be permanently installed at the German Museum of Technology, representing the first implementation in a public institution.

A mood report from an interested visitor can be read, among others, in a ["forum post at RBF"](https://www.radio-bastler.de/forum/index.php?thread/29054-cohiradia-vorf%C3%BChrung-in-berlin/&postID=318069#post318069).
