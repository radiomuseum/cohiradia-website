## Other Archives

In addition to COHIRADIA, the following archives are known to also contain interesting broadband material:

* [My DX Travel Logs](https://www.donmooredxer.com)  
  Many Latin American recordings and a few LW recordings where European stations also appear. 
* [The Radio Spectrum Archive](https://spectrumarchive.org)  
  Recordings from the USA, partly digitized analog recordings with video recorders dating back to 1986
* [Perseus WAV Files by Günter Lorenz](http://pira.fmlist.org/perseus/)  
   European archive with rather short sequences from 2009

## Interesting Other Projects
In the context of COHIRADIA, there are now some activities where recordings from our archive are used:

* [Radiobastler.de Project with fl2k](https://www.radio-bastler.de/forum/index.php?thread/27410-cohiradia-player-unter-gnu-radio/&postID=297313&highlight=fl2k#post297313)
  
  Here, the OSMO-fl2k USB-VGA converter was presented in the context of GNURadio, before a corresponding driver was integrated into the COHIWizard. Alternative hard- and software approaches for playing COHIRADIA recordings are discussed under this link. An exciting link for LINUX-savvy radio enthusiasts!
  
The following two videos show the application of the fl2k system on two old receivers by Gerald Gauert, who, among other things, supervises the exhibition [100 Years of Broadcasting](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qlJWguweo4U) at the Museumshof Chörau:
