## Installation of the Raspberry Pi 4:

To operate the ADALM2000 on a PC, libm2k must be installed. [On this page](https://wiki.analog.com/university/tools/m2k/libm2k/libm2k#windows) you will find the corresponding instructions for various operating systems.

Since the ADALM2000 option has not been extensively tested by the COHIRADIA team to date, there is unfortunately little experience with installation problems. In principle, however, the drivers could be installed under both Windows 10 and Debian 12/13.
