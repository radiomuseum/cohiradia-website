## Prepare the SD card with the OS and the COHI-Player for the Raspberry Pi:

Pre-configured, ready-to-run images with the operating system and the COHI-Player are available for the Raspberry Pi; they are suitable for both the STEMLAB and the OSMO fl2k dongle.

**Important note:** There are many different possibilities to run the COHI-Player on a Raspberry Pi. You can choose between a Raspberry Pi 4 or 5 and you can opt for different types of displays. All installations require specific configurations and the installation of the correct drivers. As there are so many possible combinations, we decided to support just very few with a pre-prepared image, which then needs no further adaptations or additional installations. The supported combinations are

(1) Raspberry Pi 4 with 3.5" display. Two displays are definitely supported: </br>
(a) official Raspberry 3.5" Touchscreen, type RPI-35LCD </br>
(b) 3.5" Touchscreen for Raspberry Pi 4, 480 x 320 Pixel 60 FPS Mini Monitor with Stylus and  Mini-ventilator for Raspberry Pi 3/4/5, offered by CUQI </br>

(2) Raspberry Pi 5 with official 7" Display, type RPI-7LCD2

For the COHI-Player+Stream mode or many simultaneous internet radio streams, the Raspberry Pi 5 is recommended (see [Documentation → Software](/en/docs/documentation/software/#cohi-player)).

Once you have made your choice, follow these steps:

**Create a new SD card  for the Raspberry Pi as follows:**

1. Insert a new SD card (at least 16 GB) into the SD card slot of your PC (if available) or into an SD card reader connected to your PC.
